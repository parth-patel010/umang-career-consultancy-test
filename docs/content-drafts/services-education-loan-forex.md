## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you provide the education loan yourselves?
No. Loans are provided by banks/NBFCs. We help you prepare and navigate the process.

### Can you guarantee loan approval?
No. Approval depends on lender policy, income, credit, collateral, and documentation.

### What is GIC and do I need it?
GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate) style proof/payment is relevant for some Canada study pathways. Requirements change — confirm for your program and visa category.

### When should I pay university tuition?
Follow the offer letter conditions and CAS/COE guidance for your destination. We’ll help you sequence payments safely.

### Can forex be done from Vadodara?
Yes — through authorised dealers/banks. We’ll guide documents commonly required.

### Does a loan improve visa chances?
Adequate, well-documented funding helps demonstrate financial capability. A loan is one possible source — not a magic approval tool. Visa decisions remain with authorities.

### Will you negotiate interest rates?
You negotiate with the lender. We can help you understand typical comparison points to ask about.
