## What Makes a Strong SOP (Quick Framework)

1. **Hook with purpose** — not clichés  
2. **Academic & experiential evidence** — projects, coursework, work, volunteering  
3. **Program fit** — modules, labs, faculty themes, learning style (researched, honest)  
4. **Career direction** — realistic near-term goals  
5. **Country/institution rationale** — practical and academic reasons  
6. **Closing** — confident, specific, forward-looking

## Common Mistakes We Help You Avoid

- Copying sample SOPs from the internet
- Overpromising PR/immigration motives in academic SOPs
- Ignoring word limits
- Listing life history without reflection
- Inconsistencies in dates, grades, or job titles
- Asking recommenders to sign a letter they did not write thoughtfully

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will you write my SOP entirely for me?
We provide structured drafting support based on your inputs. Universities expect *your* authentic voice and facts. You remain responsible for the final content.

### Can you help if English is not my strength?
Yes — we focus on clear professional English while preserving your meaning. You should still understand and be able to discuss every line (especially for interviews).

### How many revisions are included?
Confirm in your service discussion. Complex profiles often need more than one round.

### Do you write LORs yourself for professors to sign?
Best practice is recommender-authored letters. We provide guidance and structure so recommenders can write stronger, specific letters.

### Is an academic resume different from a job resume?
Often yes — education, projects, research, and relevant skills may be emphasised differently for admissions.

### Can one SOP work for every university?
Usually no. Core story can be shared, but program-specific fit paragraphs should be adapted.

### How does this connect to visa interviews?
