## Eligibility Overview (General Themes)

Dependent routes typically assess:

1. **Primary applicant’s eligible status** (student/work/other — country-specific)  
2. **Genuine relationship** (marriage/partnership evidence)  
3. **Financial capacity** to support dependents as required  
4. **Intention to comply** with dependent visa conditions  
5. **Identity, health, and character** requirements where applicable  

*Some study levels or course types may not permit dependents — verify for your destination and program.*

## Documents Checklist (Illustrative)

### Relationship evidence (examples)
- Marriage certificate
- Wedding photographs / invitations (supplementary)
- Joint financial records, if available
- Communication/travel evidence supporting relationship history
- Affidavits where relevant and truthful

### Primary applicant proofs
- Passport/visa/status documents
- Offer letter / enrollment / employment proofs as applicable
- Financial evidence covering family maintenance requirements

### Dependent identity
- Passport, photos, civil documents
- Birth certificates for child dependents
- Police/medical clearances if required

### Application consistency pack
- Matching addresses, timelines, and personal histories across forms
- Translations by accepted formats where needed

We customise checklists — do not submit irrelevant bulk documents that create confusion.

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming every student visa automatically allows a spouse
- Insufficient genuine-relationship documentation for recently married couples
- Financial proofs that cover only the primary applicant
- Contradictory marriage dates or addresses across files
- Oversharing unrelated documents while missing core proofs
- Not preparing for questions about plans, funds, and return/compliance intent

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can my spouse work on a dependent visa?
Work rights vary widely by country and visa subclass. We can share general awareness for your destination; verify on official sources.

### Should we apply together or after the student visa is approved?
It depends on country rules and risk strategy. We’ll discuss sequencing options for your case.

### We married recently — is that a problem?
Not automatically. Newer relationships may need stronger, well-organised evidence of genuineness.

### Do unmarried partners qualify?
Some countries recognise de facto/common-law partnerships with specific evidence. Many require legal marriage. Confirm for your destination.

### What about newborn or child dependents?
Possible in many categories with birth certificates and custody proofs as applicable. Rules differ.

### Can visitor visa be used instead of spouse visa?
Different purposes and conditions. Using the wrong category can cause refusals. See [/visa/visitor-visa](/visa/visitor-visa).

### Will you guarantee approval if documents are complete?
No. Completeness reduces avoidable errors; it does not control the decision.
