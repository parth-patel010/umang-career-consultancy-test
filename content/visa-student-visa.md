## Eligibility Overview (General — Not Legal Advice)

While each country sets its own rules, student visa assessments commonly look for:

1. **Genuine study intention** for a recognised program  
2. **Admission evidence** from an eligible institution  
3. **Financial capacity** to pay tuition and living costs (as defined by that country)  
4. **Language ability** where required  
5. **Health / character** requirements where applicable  
6. **Intent to comply** with visa conditions  

Your counsellor will discuss how these themes appear in *your* destination’s checklist.

## Documents Checklist (Illustrative)

*Exact lists vary. Use this as a planning framework — not an official checklist.*

### Identity & civil documents
- Valid passport (with required blank pages / validity)
- Photographs meeting embassy specifications
- Birth certificate / identity proofs as required

### Academic & admission
- Offer letter / CAS / COE / enrollment confirmation (as applicable)
- Academic transcripts and certificates
- Language test results (IELTS/PTE/others) if required
- SOP / study plan where requested

### Financial evidence (examples)
- Bank statements / loan sanction letters / sponsor affidavits
- Tuition payment receipts / deposit proofs
- GIC or similar (if applicable to your pathway)
- Income proofs of sponsor (ITR, salary slips, business proofs — as relevant)

### Other commonly requested items
- Medical examination reports (country-specific)
- Police clearance certificates (where required)
- Travel history / previous visas
- Explanation letters for gaps/backlogs/refusals (if applicable)

We help you build a **case-specific checklist** during counselling.

## Student Visa Process — Typical Flow

1. **Secure admission evidence** required for your destination  
2. **Confirm financial plan** and gather proofs  
3. **Complete online application** carefully and consistently  
4. **Pay visa fees** and schedule biometrics/VAC appointment  
5. **Attend medicals** if mandated  
6. **Submit supporting documents** as instructed  
7. **Prepare for interview** (if applicable)  
8. **Await decision** and follow passport collection / e-visa instructions  

Processing times fluctuate — verify official estimates for your country.

## Interview Preparation Themes

Officers may explore:
- Why this course and university?
- How it connects to your prior studies/work?
- Who is funding you and how?
- What are your plans after the program?
- Do you understand living costs and visa conditions?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Inconsistent dates across SOP, forms, and bank documents
- Borrowed funds parked briefly without credible history (where scrutiny is high)
- Inability to explain course modules in your own words
- Undisclosed previous refusals or visa overstays (disclose as required)
- Submitting blurry scans or incomplete translations
- Booking non-refundable tickets before decision (usually risky)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I start my student visa file?
Often immediately after you understand offer conditions and funding timeline — sometimes in parallel with final admission steps. Ask us for destination-specific sequencing.

### Can I apply with a study gap?
Possibly — many genuine students have gaps. You need a coherent explanation and supporting evidence. Assessment is case-by-case by authorities.

### Do dependents apply together?
Sometimes. Rules differ by country and program level. See [/visa/spouse-visa](/visa/spouse-visa).

### What if my visa is refused?
Review the refusal grounds, correct documentation/strategy issues, and decide on reapplication timing. We can help analyse and rebuild — without promising outcomes.

### Do you provide fake financial documents?
Absolutely not. We only support genuine documentation.

### Is travel insurance mandatory before visa?
Depends on destination. Some require proof of insurance at visa or arrival stage — we will note this in your checklist when relevant.

### Which countries do you support for student visas?
Commonly UK, Canada, USA, Australia, New Zealand, and select European study pathways — confirm your destination with us.
