## Why Students Consider Canada (General Themes Only)

We share **themes for counselling conversations** — not rankings, guarantees, or invented outcomes:

- **Program breadth** — Diploma, undergraduate, graduate certificate/diploma, and master’s pathways in many fields  
- **Campus & city choice** — Options across provinces; lifestyle and cost of living differ by location

- **Intake planning** — Multiple entry points in a year for many programs (confirm per institution)

## Intakes (General Guidance)

Many Canadian institutions offer intakes around **Fall (often the largest)**, with additional windows such as **Winter** and, for some programs, **Spring/Summer**. Treat these as **planning anchors**, not universal guarantees:

- Confirm the intake your target program actually offers  
- Work backward: tests → applications → offer → funds → visa filing  
- Avoid locking a deposit before your funding path is realistic  

Intake calendars differ by college/university and course. Your counsellor will map a timeline for your preferred season.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Canada good for study after Class 12?
Often yes for diploma or undergraduate pathways — when academics, language readiness, and budget align. Start with [/resources/career-after-12th](/resources/career-after-12th) and a counselling session.

### Do I need IELTS to study in Canada from Vadodara?
Many programs require IELTS, PTE, or other English evidence; limited alternatives/waivers exist case-by-case. We help with **language-score planning**. We do **not** provide IELTS coaching.

### How much does it cost to study in Canada?
Costs vary widely by city, institution, and program length. We discuss realistic **bands after shortlisting** — we do not publish invented fee tables as facts.

### What intakes should I target?
Many students plan around Fall; Winter (and sometimes Spring/Summer) may work depending on the course. Confirm per institution and work backward from test and funding dates.

### Can Umang help with the Canada student visa?
Yes — with **document organisation, consistency checks, and interview preparation themes**. This is guidance, **not legal advice**, and **not a guarantee** of approval. See [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa).

### Canada vs UK vs Australia — which is better?
None is universally “better.” Compare course fit, duration, budget, and visa readiness. Use [/destinations](/destinations) and counselling to decide.

### Do you have official partner universities in Canada?
We do **not** invent “official partner” lists as marketing claims. We shortlist institutions that fit your profile and goals through transparent counselling.
