## The 7-Step Journey

### Step 1 — Free discovery counselling

**Goal:** Understand your goals, academics, budget band, preferred countries, and intake timing.

We listen first. You share marksheets (or latest results), passport status, family decision timeline, and any hard constraints (budget ceiling, city preference, work experience). We outline whether study-abroad timing looks realistic for your preferred season — or what needs strengthening first.

→ Book: [/contact](/contact) · Career counselling deep page: [/services/career-counselling](/services/career-counselling)

### Step 2 — Profile assessment

**Goal:** Map strengths, gaps, language-score planning, and financial readiness.

We review academics, gaps/backlogs (if any), English readiness, and funding paths (savings, sponsor, loan, or mix). We help set **target score bands against shortlisted courses** — this is **score planning only**. We do **not** provide IELTS coaching.

Honest outcome of this step: a clear “ready / almost ready / strengthen first” view — not a sales pitch for a single country.

### Step 3 — Shortlist & strategy

**Goal:** Build a practical course/university (or visa-category) plan.

Based on your profile, we shortlist options that fit academics, budget, and intake — across destinations you are seriously considering. We do **not** invent official partner-university marketing lists. Shortlisting is **profile-based**. See [/universities](/universities) and [/services/university-admission](/services/university-admission).

Destination pages for deeper country context: [/destinations/canada](/destinations/canada) · [/destinations/uk](/destinations/uk) · [/destinations/australia](/destinations/australia) · [/destinations/usa](/destinations/usa) · [/destinations/new-zealand](/destinations/new-zealand)

### Step 4 — Applications & documents

**Goal:** Submit organised applications with a consistent academic narrative.

We guide application sequencing, document checklists, and coordination of SOP / LOR / resume support so your story stays consistent across schools. Deadlines and offer conditions are tracked so deposits are not rushed blindly.

→ [/services/university-admission](/services/university-admission) · [/services/sop-lor-resume](/services/sop-lor-resume) · SOP guide: [/resources/sop-writing-guide](/resources/sop-writing-guide)

### Step 5 — Funding & forex guidance

**Goal:** Align fee payment and remittance practicalities with your offer timeline.

We orient education-loan conversations and forex / remittance pointers so tuition and related payments are planned — not improvised the week of a deadline. Exact loan products and bank rules vary; we do not invent fixed fee tables as facts.

→ [/services/education-loan-forex](/services/education-loan-forex)

### Step 6 — Visa readiness

**Goal:** Organise evidence, forms, and interview themes for your student (or family) visa category.

We help with checklists, consistency checks (names, dates, course details), and interview preparation themes. This is **guidance, not legal advice**. Rules change; always verify on official government sources. **Umang Career Consultancy does not guarantee visa approval** — decisions rest solely with immigration / consular authorities.

→ [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa) · Hub: [/visa](/visa) · Interview tips: [/resources/visa-interview-tips](/resources/visa-interview-tips)

### Step 7 — Pre-departure orientation

**Goal:** Reduce last-minute travel stress after a visa outcome.

We cover practical basics: travel documents to carry, packing themes, arrival checklist ideas, and who to contact for common early questions. This step supports a calm transition — it is not a substitute for university arrival instructions.

## Timelines — Honest Planning Notes

- **Ideal start:** Begin counselling **several months** before your target intake — language tests and funding often drive the calendar.  
- **Rush cases:** Possible only when academics, tests, and funds are already strong; we will say so if a preferred intake is unrealistic.  
- **No universal “X weeks guarantee”:** University processing and visa outcomes are outside any consultancy’s control.  
- **Germany:** Selective discussion in counselling only; no thin Germany destination page in this content batch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the first counselling session free?
Contact us to confirm the current counselling policy. Many students begin with an initial discussion to understand fit and next steps. Call [+91 9173186109](tel:+919173186109).

### Do I have to follow all seven steps?
The sequence is a standard roadmap. Some students join mid-journey (e.g. already have an offer and need visa readiness). We adapt — we do not force unnecessary steps.

### Can parents attend counselling?
Yes. Many Vadodara families prefer joint sessions for budget and timeline clarity. Visit our office or join a scheduled call.

### Where does the student visa sit in the process?
Typically after an offer (and funding plan) are clear enough to file. See Step 6 and [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa).

### Do you guarantee admission or visa success?
No. We provide organised guidance and preparation. Universities and immigration authorities make final decisions.

### How do I start?
Call, WhatsApp, email, or submit the Lite enquire form on [/contact](/contact). Bring marksheets and a rough budget if visiting in person.
