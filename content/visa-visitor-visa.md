## Eligibility Overview (General)

Visitor assessments commonly examine:

1. **Genuine temporary visit purpose**  
2. **Funds for the trip** without unauthorised work  
3. **Ties to home country** (family, employment, property, responsibilities)  
4. **Travel history** and prior compliance (if any)  
5. **Invitation details** when visiting family/friends  
6. **Willingness to leave** before visa expiry  

This is a simplified overview — not a determination of your eligibility.

## Documents Checklist (Illustrative)

*Confirm official lists for your destination.*

### Core identity
- Passport
- Photos as per specification
- Application form printouts / confirmations

### Purpose of visit
- Cover letter explaining trip purpose and duration
- Invitation letter (if visiting family/friends)
- Host’s status documents (visa/PR/passport copies — as applicable)
- Hotel bookings / day-wise itinerary (flexible, honest)
- Event invitations (wedding cards, conference notes) if relevant

### Financials
- Bank statements
- ITR / income proofs
- Sponsor letter + sponsor finances (if sponsored)
- Trip cost estimate

### Ties to India / home country
- Employment letter & leave approval
- Business registration proofs
- Property documents (if helpful)
- Family dependent details where relevant

### Other
- Previous visas/travel history
- Marriage certificate / relationship proofs when travelling as a family

## Common Mistakes

- Overly rigid non-refundable bookings before approval
- Invitation letters that contradict application answers
- Inflated trip duration without funds to match
- Ignoring previous refusal explanations
- Poor-quality scans and mismatched spellings of names
- Suggesting work intentions on a visitor application

## Tips for Parents Visiting Students

- Show clear student status documents of the child abroad
- Explain who pays for the trip and living costs during visit
- Keep duration realistic for the stated purpose
- Demonstrate strong reasons to return to India
- Align school/university holiday timing if relevant

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I convert a visitor visa to student/work later?
Do not assume this is possible or advisable. Rules are strict and misuse can harm future applications. Ask during counselling for general awareness — not as a plan.

### How much bank balance do I need?
There is rarely a single universal number. Officers assess overall credibility — income, history, trip cost, and ties. We’ll help you present a coherent picture.

### Is an invitation mandatory?
Not always, but it helps for family visits. Tourism-only trips may rely more on itinerary and funds.

### What if I was refused before?
Disclose as required, address the reasons, and strengthen documentation. Reapplication without fixes often repeats outcomes.

### Do you help with USA B1/B2, UK Standard Visitor, Schengen, Canada visitor, Australia visitor, etc.?
Share your destination — we will confirm support for that category and current documentation themes.

### Should employed applicants submit leave letters?
Often helpful as evidence of ties and approved absence. Requirements vary.

### Can my child abroad sponsor me?
Sponsorship can help, but you may still need to show overall credibility and return ties. Case-specific.
