## Intakes (General Guidance)

Many U.S. institutions organise around a major **Fall** intake, with **Spring** (and sometimes summer or rolling) starts for select programs. Treat these as **planning anchors**, not universal guarantees:

- Confirm the intake your target program actually offers  
- Work backward: tests → essays/LOR → applications → offers → I-20 themes → funds → visa filing  
- Account for application fee budgets across multiple schools  
- Avoid locking deposits before your funding path is realistic  

Intake calendars and deadlines differ widely. Your counsellor will map a timeline for your preferred season.

- Valid passport with sufficient validity  
- Academic transcripts and certificates (Class 10/12, graduation, etc. as applicable)

- Offer / admission letter and Form I-20 themes as issued by the institution

- Names, dates, and course details matching across forms

## Common Mistakes Vadodara Applicants Make

1. **Choosing the USA for prestige alone** — without checking course fit, competitiveness, or budget  
2. **Underestimating application workload** — essays, LORs, and deadlines across multiple schools  
3. **Booking language or standardised tests late** — then missing preferred intakes  
4. **Comparing only first-year tuition** — ignoring full program + living + insurance costs  
5. **Inconsistent SOP vs interview answers** — weakens genuine-student narrative  
6. **Assuming coaching equals counselling** — we provide **score planning only**, not IELTS coaching  
7. **Treating visa as paperwork-only** — purpose, funds, and consistency all matter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the USA good for study after Class 12?
Often discussed for undergraduate pathways — when academics, essays, tests (where required), and budget align. Start with [/resources/career-after-12th](/resources/career-after-12th) and a counselling session.

### Do I need IELTS or TOEFL to study in the USA from Vadodara?
Many programs require IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or other English evidence; limited alternatives/waivers exist case-by-case. Some shortlists also discuss GRE/GMAT or other tests. We help with **language-score planning**. We do **not** provide IELTS coaching.

### How much does it cost to study in the USA?
Costs vary widely by institution type, location, and program length. We discuss realistic **bands after shortlisting** — we do not publish invented fee tables as facts.

### What intakes should I target?
Many students plan around Fall; Spring (and other starts) may work depending on the course. Confirm per institution and work backward from test, essay, and funding dates.

### Can Umang help with the USA student visa?
Yes — with **document organisation, consistency checks, and interview preparation themes**. This is guidance, **not legal advice**, and **not a guarantee** of approval. See [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa).

### USA vs Canada vs UK — which is better?
None is universally “better.” Compare course fit, duration, application load, budget, and visa readiness. Use [/destinations](/destinations) and counselling to decide.
