## Step 3: India vs Study Abroad Decision Framework

### Study in India may fit if:
- You want highly competitive national entrance pathways you’re prepared for
- Budget needs to stay tightly controlled
- You prefer staying closer to family for UG years

### Study abroad may fit if:
- Your target specialisation/teaching style is stronger overseas for your goals
- Family can sustain multi-year costs responsibly
- You are ready for independent living and language requirements
- You have time to plan tests, applications, and visas

## Step 4: If Considering Abroad After 12th

### Timeline (typical)
- Month 0–1: counselling + shortlist direction

- Month 2–6: applications & SOP/resume  
- After offers: funding + [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa)  

Exact calendars depend on country and intake.

- 10th & 12th marksheets (and predicted scores if applicable)

## Step 5: Avoid These After-12th Traps

1. Choosing a course only because friends did  
2. Ignoring total abroad cost beyond tuition posters  
3. Paying agents before understanding the plan  
4. Skipping language test planning until too late  
5. Treating rankings as the only quality signal  
6. Assuming every abroad course leads to easy PR (it doesn’t)

## Conversation Script for Parents & Students

Use this in a family meeting:

1. What are our top 2 goals for the next 4 years?  
2. What is our realistic annual budget?  
3. Are we open to diploma/pathway routes if needed?  
4. Which cities/countries feel acceptable for safety & lifestyle?  
5. What is Plan B if the first intake doesn’t work?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My 12th scores are average — can I still go abroad?
Possibly, through careful country/course selection or pathway programs. Profile review required.

### Is foundation/pathway a waste of time?
Not if it is the realistic bridge to your degree goals — evaluate cost and progression conditions.

### Should I take a drop year?
Only with a structured plan (tests, portfolio, skill-building). Idle drop years are harder to explain later.

### Can I change fields later?
Sometimes via master’s or lateral pathways — easier when UG choices aren’t completely mismatched.

### When should I get a passport?
As early as possible if abroad is on the table.
