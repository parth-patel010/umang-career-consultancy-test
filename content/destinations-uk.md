## Why Students Consider the UK (General Themes Only)

Themes we discuss in counselling — **not rankings, success rates, or guarantees**:

- **Specialised course catalogues** — Many taught postgraduate and undergraduate options across fields  
- **Program duration awareness** — Taught master’s often discussed for relatively focused timelines (confirm per course)  
- **City & campus variety** — Different living costs and lifestyles by location  
- **Clear application seasons** — Strong September intake culture, with additional windows for some programs  
- **Documentation discipline** — Offer/CAS-style admission evidence and consistent study purpose matter for visa readiness  

The UK is **not** automatically cheaper or “easier.” Tuition, living costs, and entry requirements must be stress-tested against your profile and funding plan.

## Intakes (General Guidance)

Many UK programs centre on a **September / autumn** start. Some courses also offer **January** (or other) intakes. Use these as planning anchors:

- Confirm whether *your* course runs in your preferred season  
- Reverse-plan: language test → applications → offer → funds → visa filing  
- Do not treat social-media “last-minute UK” stories as a planning method  

Your counsellor will build a calendar around the intake that is realistic for your documents and finances.

Often yes (or another accepted English test). Some applicants explore alternatives/waivers case-by-case. We help with **target-score planning**; we do **not** provide IELTS coaching.

September / autumn is the largest for many courses; January and other starts exist for some programs. Confirm for your shortlist.
