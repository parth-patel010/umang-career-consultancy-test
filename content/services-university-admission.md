## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many universities should I apply to?
Enough to balance ambition and safety — typically a focused set rather than dozens. We’ll recommend based on your profile and budget for application fees.

### Do you apply only to “top 100” universities?
Prestige matters to some students; employability, cost, and visa practicality matter too. We balance what *you* value.

### What if my scores are average?
Many pathways exist across destinations. We discuss realistic options, pathway programs, or profile-building where suitable.

### Can you help with scholarships?
We can guide you on where to look and how to present a stronger scholarship narrative. Scholarship awards are never guaranteed.

### Do I need IELTS/PTE before shortlisting?
Not always — but language requirements affect eligibility. We’ll map test timing into your plan.

### What happens after I get an offer?
Deposit decisions, financial proof planning, CAS/COE steps (as applicable), then student visa preparation.

### Are you a university representative for every institution?
Treat any “partner” claims carefully. We guide applications based on public eligibility criteria and your goals; confirm any official representation separately if claimed.
