- **Pathway flexibility** — Diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate options depending on field and entry profile  
- **Quality-of-life conversations** — Lifestyle and study environment themes families often raise in counselling

## Intakes (General Guidance)

New Zealand institutions commonly organise teaching around major periods such as **Semester 1 (often early in the calendar year)** and **Semester 2 (mid-year)**, with additional starts for some programs. Treat these as planning anchors:

- Confirm intakes for *your* shortlisted courses  
- Work backward: language test → applications → offer → funds → visa filing  
- Avoid last-minute deposits without a funding and document plan  

Your counsellor will map a realistic calendar for your preferred start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Zealand good for study after Class 12?
Often yes for diploma or undergraduate pathways — when academics, language readiness, and budget align. Start with [/resources/career-after-12th](/resources/career-after-12th) and a counselling session.

### Do I need IELTS to study in New Zealand from Vadodara?
Many programs require IELTS, PTE, or other English evidence; limited alternatives/waivers exist case-by-case. We help with **language-score planning**. We do **not** provide IELTS coaching.

### How much does it cost to study in New Zealand?
Costs vary by city, institution, and program length. We discuss realistic **bands after shortlisting** — we do not publish invented fee tables as facts.

### What intakes should I target?
Many students plan around Semester 1 or Semester 2 starts. Confirm per institution and work backward from test and funding dates.

### Can Umang help with the New Zealand student visa?
Yes — with **document organisation, consistency checks, and interview preparation themes**. This is guidance, **not legal advice**, and **not a guarantee** of approval. See [/visa/student-visa](/visa/student-visa).

### New Zealand vs Australia — which is better?
Neither is universally “better.” Compare course fit, campus scale, budget, and visa readiness. Use [/destinations](/destinations) and counselling to decide.

### Do you have official partner universities in New Zealand?
We do **not** invent “official partner” lists as marketing claims. We shortlist institutions that fit your profile and goals through transparent counselling. See also [/universities](/universities).
