## Quick Planning Checklist

Use this before your first counselling session:

1. List your preferred fields (even if uncertain)  
2. Note budget range (annual tuition + living comfort zone)  
3. Gather recent marksheets and passport status  
4. Decide target intake (month/year)  
5. Write 5 career/life goals in plain language  
6. List questions for your counsellor
