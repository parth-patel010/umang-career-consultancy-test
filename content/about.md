## Our Story

Umang Career Consultancy is a Vadodara-based education and visa guidance practice helping students and families navigate overseas education and travel pathways with clarity.

We started with a simple observation: families often receive fragmented advice — one person for “colleges,” another for “visas,” and little coordination between the two. That fragmentation creates inconsistent stories, rushed documents, and avoidable stress.

Our approach is to build **one coherent roadmap** — from career conversations and university applications to documentation, funding orientation, and visa readiness.

## Our Values

### Clarity over hype
We prefer plain explanations of costs, timelines, and risks over glamorous promises.

### Profile-first planning
Your academics, budget, and goals drive the shortlist — not a fixed brochure.

### Documentation discipline
Consistent files reduce preventable delays.

### Family-inclusive counselling
Parents are decision partners. We welcome joint sessions.

### Ethical boundaries
We do not support forged documents or guaranteed-approval claims.

## Our Approach (How We Work)

1. **Listen** — goals, constraints, family context  
2. **Diagnose** — profile strengths and gaps  
3. **Recommend** — realistic pathways with pros/cons  
4. **Execute** — applications and document readiness  
5. **Prepare** — visa file organisation and interview practice  
6. **Support** — pre-departure orientation basics
